## D8 course-scope decision (2026-08-17)

The first course release uses two explicit source contracts:

- `starryos_merged_baseline`: an upstream merged, fixed 40-character commit/tree selected by the teacher; it supplies required executable practice and public release evidence.
- `cgroup_project_reference`: the fixed cgroup analysis source `fc80b868fb3640efe8997994de42c1aee8fd74cb` / tree `832ce21ea6fdf32a8639c576cc97a137c2d14dcc`; it supplies the complete project map, source facts and boundary/design review.

`implementation_state` (`present/partial/absent/conflicting/unresolved`) is separate from teaching maturity. Public-core, project-reference and cgroup-specialization findings are evaluated in separate scopes. Partial or absent cgroup connections may be reviewed as project-reference material, but cannot be advertised as effective resource control or required executable evidence. The only outstanding source decision is the public merged baseline identity; no automatic move to `dev`, `main` or current `HEAD` is allowed.
